Clinical safety evidence, under version control

Turva manages hazards, risk assessments, mitigations and safety cases for healthcare IT systems, with an audit trail that can withstand regulatory scrutiny.

Each Clinical Safety Management File is a Git repository, so every change is attributed, timestamped and permanent. You never need to touch Git: Turva does that for you.

Turva supports the DCB0129 and DCB0160 clinical risk management processes. It does not make clinical decisions, does not process patient data, and does not replace a Clinical Safety Officer.
