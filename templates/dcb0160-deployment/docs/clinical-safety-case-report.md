## 3. Safety argument

This report argues that the deployment of {{ name }} within {{ organisation }} is acceptably safe for its intended use, on the following grounds:

1. The hazards arising from this deployment have been systematically identified, and are recorded in the [hazard log](hazards/index.md)
2. Each hazard has been assessed for severity and likelihood, with risk level derived consistently rather than assigned
3. Controls have been specified for each hazard, preferring design and technical controls over procedural ones
4. Residual risk has been reassessed after those controls, and no hazard remains at a risk level requiring mandatory elimination
5. Evidence of implementation is recorded against each control
6. Arrangements are in place to detect and respond to safety issues after go-live
